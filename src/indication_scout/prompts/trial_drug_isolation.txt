Read the arms and their interventions and work out which active agents each arm receives. An intervention can contain

Not active agents for this question: placebo, sham, no treatment, observation, diet, exercise, lifestyle programs,
counseling and other behavioral interventions. Background therapy is also not counted: an established standard
treatment for the condition (for example standard chemotherapy, radiotherapy, hormone therapy, insulin in type 1
diabetes) that every arm receives, or that a single-arm trial adds THIS drug to.

- "isolated": any one of these holds.
  * An arm gives THIS drug as its only active agent (single-arm trials included).
  * Two arms differ only by THIS drug: drug vs placebo or no treatment, drug + X vs X, drug + X vs placebo + X.
  * Two arms differ only by swapping THIS drug for another treatment on an otherwise identical regimen
    (drug + X vs Y + X, or drug vs another drug): a head-to-head comparison.
  Check every pair of arms, not only experimental vs comparator.
- "combination_only": every arm that gives THIS drug also gives another active agent that is not background therapy,
  and no pair of arms isolates THIS drug as above.
- "absent": no arm gives THIS drug (for example a withdrawal or discontinuation study).
- "unclear": arms are not reported and the title, interventions and descriptions do not show the design.
